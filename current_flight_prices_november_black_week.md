## 4. Crucial Booking Trick for 4–7 Passengers ("Fare Bucket Split")

Airlines allocate only **2 to 4 seats** in the lowest Black Week fare bucket on Svalbard flights. If only 3 seats exist at `1,199 NOK` and you search for **5 passengers** in one booking, the system automatically bumps **all 5 passengers** to the higher fare bucket (e.g., `1,699 NOK`).

**Always do this before paying:**
1. Search for **`1 adult`** first to see the true floor price on May 3 and May 7.
2. Search for your full group (**`4` or `5 adults`**).
3. If the per-person price jumps, **split the purchase into two bookings** (e.g., 2–3 tickets at the lower price + 2 tickets at the next tier) to save 300–600 PLN across the group!
