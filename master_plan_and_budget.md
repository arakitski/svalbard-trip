### Day-by-Day Schedule Breakdown

#### **Day 1: Flight In & Arctic Acclimatization**
* **Morning (06:15 – 13:20):** Depart Warsaw Chopin (`WAW`), smooth 2h transfer at Oslo Gardermoen (`OSL`), land at Svalbard Airport (`LYR`) at 13:20.
* **Afternoon:** Take `Flybussen` shuttle to hotel. Check-in, pick up rented heavy Arctic outerwear (boots, suits, mitts) and complete rental paperwork at *Ingeniør G. Paulsen (IGP)*.
* **Late Afternoon:** Inspect snowmobiles, verify firearms licensing (European Firearms Pass / Governor permit), and conduct brief safety orientation.
* **Evening:** Group welcome dinner at *Kroa* or *Svalbar* (reindeer burgers, Arctic cod). Experience the 24-hour Midnight Sun.

#### **Day 2: Independent Snowmobile Expedition (Adventdalen $\rightarrow$ Sassendalen $\rightarrow$ Fredheim)**
* **Morning (09:00):** Gear up, load spare fuel, GPS tracker (Garmin inReach), and 2 rifles onto 3 touring snowmobiles (2 riders per sled or individual).
* **Midday:** Ride through the broad, crevasse-free valley of **Adventdalen**, passing historical coal mine entrances and climbing into **Sassendalen**. Stop at **Villa Fredheim** (the historic coastal cabin of legendary trapper Hilmar Nøis) overlooking Tempelfjorden.
* **Lunch:** Hot expedition meals and coffee brewed on portable stoves in the wilderness.
* **Afternoon (16:30):** Return sleds to Longyearbyen, refuel, and return weapons.
* **Evening:** Tour and craft beer tasting at **Svalbard Brewery** (*Svalbard Bryggeri*, world’s northernmost craft brewery).

#### **Day 3: Dog Sledding & Glacier Ice Cave Overnight**
* **Morning (09:30 – 13:00):** Half-day dog sledding safari through Bolterdalen. Each person drives their own team of Alaskan Huskies.
* **Afternoon (14:30):** Rendezvous with the specialized glacier guide from *Svalbard Wildlife Expeditions*. Hike/snowshoe up to Larsbreen or Longyearbreen glacier (~400m elevation gain) carrying personal overnight gear.
* **Evening:** Descend ladders into the subterranean glacier ice cave. Enjoy an expedition dinner inside the natural ice hall surrounded by blue meltwater sculptures.
* **Night:** Sleep inside the glacier on high-insulation expedition mats in $-30^\circ\text{C}$ sleeping bags (ambient cave temperature is a steady $-4^\circ\text{C}$ with zero wind). Guide stands polar bear watch at the entrance.

#### **Day 4: Glacier Descent, Warm Sauna & Arctic Celebration**
* **Morning (08:30 – 11:30):** Hot breakfast and coffee inside the ice cave. Pack up and hike down the glacier moraine back into Longyearbyen.
* **Midday:** Check back into hotel rooms, hot showers, sauna, and well-earned relaxation.
* **Afternoon (Optional):** 3-hour silent hybrid-electric catamaran cruise on Isfjorden (*Brim Explorer*) to spot walruses, seals, and coastal bird colonies.
* **Evening (20:00):** Expedition celebration banquet at **Huset** or **Gruvelageret** (historic Arctic fine dining in an old mining depot).

#### **Day 5: Souvenir Stroll & Flight Home**
* **Morning:** Duty-free tax-free shopping on Longyearbyen’s pedestrian street (duty-free spirits, wool sweaters, outdoor equipment). Visit the **Svalbard Museum** and **North Pole Expedition Museum**.
* **Midday (12:05 / 12:45):** Board the `Flybussen` shuttle outside the hotel.
* **Afternoon & Evening:** Flight `LYR` $\rightarrow$ `OSL` $\rightarrow$ `WAW` (departing 14:05/14:45, arriving back in Warsaw late evening).

2. **Late November (Black Friday / Cyber Week):**

   * **Action:** Reserve the **Glacier Ice Cave Overnight** with *Svalbard Wildlife Expeditions* (strict 6–8 person limit means you must secure the date early).

4. **February – March:**
   * **Action:** Reserve 3 snowmobiles with *Ingeniør G. Paulsen (IGP)*.
   * **Action:** Firearms documentation:
     * Check if group member has an **EFP (Europejska Karta Broni Palnej)**.
     * If not, apply online to the Governor of Svalbard (*Sysselmesteren*) with a clean police certificate translated into English.
5. **April (Final Prep):**
   * Verify cold-weather layering gear (merino wool, windproof shells, high-quality sleep masks for 24h sunlight).
