# Svalbard Arctic Expeditions & Activities Catalog (1–2 Days)
*Curated from Official Tourist Resources ([en.visitsvalbard.com](https://en.visitsvalbard.com))*
*Target Window: Late April – Early May | Target Group: 5–6 Active Mountain Hikers*

---

## 1. Overview & Early May Season Profile

Early May is Svalbard's famous **"Sunny Winter" / Spring Transition**:
* **24-Hour Midnight Sun:** The sun never sets (unlimited daylight for late-night or early-morning adventures).
* **Firm Valley & Glacier Snowpack:** Ideal for long-range snowmobiling, dogsledding, and glacier ascents before the mid-May thaw.
* **Fjord Ice Opening (May 1st):** The summer boat season officially launches on May 1st, meaning you can experience **both snow/ice expeditions and marine boat safaris** in the same trip.

## 2. 1-Day Activities (4 to 10 Hours)

### 2.1 High-Endurance Snowmobile Safaris

#### 1. The East Coast Expedition to Mohnbukta (The Polar Bear Realm)
* **Operator:** *Svalbard Wildlife Expeditions*, *Spitzbergen Adventures*, *Hurtigruten Svalbard*
* **Duration:** 8 – 10 hours (~190 – 210 km round-trip)
* **Difficulty:** High (physically demanding, continuous driving across arctic terrain)
* **What you do:** Ride east from Longyearbyen through Adventdalen and Sassendalen, crossing massive glacier passes to reach the frozen Barents Sea at Mohnbukta. Look across endless sea pack ice and calving glacier walls. This is Spitsbergen's prime polar bear habitat where ringed seals pup on the ice.
* **Approx. Price:** **~3,900 – 4,600 NOK** (~1,470 – 1,740 PLN / ~€340 – €400) per driver

#### 2. Tempelfjorden & Villa Fredheim (Trapper History & Glacier Front)
* **Operator:** *Better Moments*, *Svalbard Adventures*, *Poli Arctici*
* **Duration:** 7 – 8 hours (~140 – 160 km round-trip)
* **Difficulty:** Moderate (great for self-drive experience or guided convoy)
* **What you do:** Ride to Tempelfjorden and visit historic Villa Fredheim, home of legendary Norwegian trapper Hilmar Nøis (who survived 38 Arctic winters). Continue across the fjord (ice permitting) toward the jagged blue wall of the Tuna Glacier.
* **Approx. Price:** **~2,800 – 3,400 NOK** (~1,060 – 1,280 PLN / ~€245 – €295) per driver

#### 3. Fjord Ice Edge & Abandoned Soviet Mining at Colesbay
* **Operator:** *Gjestehuset 102 partners*, *Snowfox Travel*
* **Duration:** 5 – 7 hours (~80 – 100 km)
* **Difficulty:** Moderate
* **What you do:** Ride along frozen river deltas and mountain valleys to the desolate Soviet ghost settlement of Colesbay and the Rusanov memorial cabin on the ice-choked coast.
* **Approx. Price:** **~2,200 – 2,700 NOK** (~830 – 1,020 PLN / ~€190 – €235)

### 2.2 Dog Sledding (Husky Mushing)

#### 4. Full-Day Husky Mushing into Bolterdalen & Scott Turner Glacier
* **Operator:** *Green Dog Svalbard*, *Svalbard Husky*, *Basecamp Explorer*
* **Duration:** 6 – 8 hours
* **Difficulty:** Moderate to Active (you actively drive your own 5–6 dog sled team)
* **What you do:** Harness your team at the kennel and mush deep into the silence of Bolterdalen. Ascend onto the Scott Turner Glacier, explore natural blue meltwater ice channels, and eat lunch inside an expedition tent or ice cave.
* **Approx. Price:** **~2,400 – 2,900 NOK** (~900 – 1,100 PLN / ~€210 – €250) per person

#### 5. Half-Day Husky Sledding Quick Rush
* **Operator:** *Green Dog Svalbard*, *Svalbard Husky*
* **Duration:** 4 hours (Morning 09:00 or Afternoon 14:00)
* **Difficulty:** Moderate
* **What you do:** High-speed dogsled run through the wide Arctic valleys. Ideal for combining with an afternoon boat cruise or evening hike.
* **Approx. Price:** **~1,800 – 2,100 NOK** (~680 – 790 PLN / ~€155 – €185) per person

---

### 2.3 Fjord & Marine Boat Safaris (Open May 1st!)

#### 6. Silent Hybrid-Electric Fjord & Glacier Cruise
* **Operator:** *Brim Explorer*
* **Duration:** 4 hours (departing 14:30) or 6.5 hours (morning)
* **Difficulty:** Relaxed / High Scenic Impact
* **What you do:** Sail silently on an electric-hybrid catamaran along the ice edge of Isfjorden. Approaching calving glacier walls without motor noise allows close encounters with minke whales, belugas, bearded seals, and thousands of returning sea birds on the cliffs.
* **Approx. Price:** **~1,450 – 1,950 NOK** (~550 – 740 PLN / ~€125 – €170) per person

#### 7. "Two Tons of Happiness" Walrus Safari by Closed Expedition RIB
* **Operator:** *Better Moments*
* **Duration:** 5 hours (daily starting May 1st)
* **Difficulty:** Active (high-speed marine navigation)
* **What you do:** Speed across Isfjorden in a heated, closed cabin RIB boat toward Borebukta or Poolepynten, where massive walrus haul-outs rest on ice floes and gravel banks.
* **Approx. Price:** **~2,400 – 2,900 NOK** (~900 – 1,100 PLN / ~€210 – €250) per person

#### 8. "Catch of the Day" Ice Edge Wildlife Safari
* **Operator:** *Better Moments*
* **Duration:** 5 hours
* **Difficulty:** Active
* **What you do:** Navigate dynamically along the sea ice edge where spring marine life congregates (seals basking on floes, marine birds, and occasionally polar bears hunting on the ice boundary).
* **Approx. Price:** **~2,300 – 2,800 NOK** (~870 – 1,060 PLN / ~€200 – €245) per person

### 2.4 Mountain Ridge & Glacier Treks (For Mountain Hikers)

#### 9. Summit Trek to Trollsteinen (850m) via Larsbreen Glacier
* **Operator:** *Svalbard Wildlife Expeditions*
* **Duration:** 7 hours (~10 km, ~800m elevation gain)
* **Difficulty:** Demanding / Experienced Hikers (Grade 4/5)
* **What you do:** Strap on crampons/snowshoes, cross the moraine and active Larsbreen glacier, and scramble along the razor-sharp mountain ridge to the "Troll Stone" summit. Unobstructed 360-degree panorama of Spitsbergen's highest peaks and frozen fjords.
* **Approx. Price:** **~1,350 – 1,650 NOK** (~510 – 625 PLN / ~€118 – €145) per person

#### 10. "Between the Glaciers" Hike to Sarkofagen Ridge (513m)
* **Operator:** *Svalbard Wildlife Expeditions*, *Spitzbergen Adventures*
* **Duration:** 5 – 6 hours (~500m elevation gain)
* **Difficulty:** Moderate to Demanding (Grade 3/5)
* **What you do:** Ascend between the Longyearbreen and Larsbreen glaciers to the sarcophagus-shaped summit ridge. On the return descent down the glacier moraine, search for 60-million-year-old fossilized leaves.
* **Approx. Price:** **~1,050 – 1,350 NOK** (~395 – 510 PLN / ~€92 – €118) per person

#### 11. Subterranean Ice Cave Half-Day Trek (Inside Longyearbreen)
* **Operator:** *Svalbard Wildlife Expeditions*, *Svalbard Adventures*
* **Duration:** 5 hours (Snowcat or hike to glacier entrance)
* **Difficulty:** Moderate (requires crawling/scrambling in ice caverns)
* **What you do:** Enter a secret opening atop the glacier into a subterranean frozen labyrinth of blue ice, meltwater tubes, and glistening hoarfrost crystals.
* **Approx. Price:** **~1,150 – 1,450 NOK** (~435 – 550 PLN / ~€100 – €125) per person

---

### 2.5 Historic & Deep Underground Exploration

#### 12. Gruve 3 (Mine 3) Deep Underground Coal Mine Expedition
* **Operator:** *Store Norske / Gruve 3 AS*
* **Duration:** 3 hours
* **Difficulty:** Low to Moderate (crawling through low shafts optional)
* **What you do:** Put on miner overalls, helmet, and headlamp, and step into an authentic frozen coal mine preserved exactly as the miners left it when operations ceased. Walk 300 meters deep into the permafrost mountain to see cold-war machinery, tracks, and miner workshops.
* **Approx. Price:** **~850 – 990 NOK** (~320 – 375 PLN / ~€75 – €85) per person

### 13. "Sleep Inside a Glacier" (Ice Cave Overnight Expedition)
* **Operator:** *Svalbard Wildlife Expeditions*
* **Duration:** 2 Days / 1 Night (Starts Day 1 at 15:00, returns Day 2 at 11:30)
* **Format:** Guide-led alpine trek with expedition backpacks up Larsbreen or Longyearbreen glacier
* **The Experience:**
  * Descend ladders into the glacier interior.
  * Dinner cooked on camp stoves inside a natural crystal ice cathedral.
  * Sleep in $-30^\circ\text{C}$ expedition sleeping bags on high-R-value insulated pads.
  * Total silence, zero wind, steady $-3^\circ\text{C}$ to $-5^\circ\text{C}$ ambient cave temperature.
  * Guide remains on armed polar bear watch at the cave entrance.
* **Approx. Price:** **~3,800 – 4,800 NOK** (~1,430 – 1,800 PLN / ~€330 – €415) per person (includes technical gear, sleeping bags, dinner, breakfast, guide)

### 14. 2-Day Dog Sledding Adventure to Wilderness Cabin Among Glaciers
* **Operator:** *Arctic Husky Travellers* / *Green Dog Svalbard*
* **Duration:** 2 Days / 1 Night
* **Format:** Drive your own 6-dog sled team across mountain passes and valleys to a remote off-grid cabin in Tverrdalen.
* **The Experience:**
  * Day 1: Mush 30–40 km over snow-covered passes and glaciers, visiting natural ice caves en route.
  * Evening: Unharness and care for your husky pack (who act as the camp's polar bear alarm). Enjoy a hot wood-fired sauna and home-cooked dinner in an off-grid frontier cabin with no cellular reception or electricity.
  * Day 2: Mush an alternate scenic route back across Foxdalen to Longyearbyen.
* **Approx. Price:** **~7,500 – 9,800 NOK** (~2,800 – 3,700 PLN / ~€650 – €850) per person (all-inclusive)

### 15. 2-Day East Coast Snowmobile Expedition & Wilderness Cabin Escape
* **Operator:** *Svalbard Wildlife Expeditions*, *Hurtigruten Svalbard*
* **Duration:** 2 Days / 1 Night (~300 km total riding)
* **Format:** High-powered snowmobile expedition crossing Spitsbergen to the Siberian pack ice of the East Coast.
* **The Experience:**
  * Day 1: Ride deep through Sassendalen, climbing glaciers to reach the wild East Coast. Search for polar bears on the sea ice.
  * Overnight: Sleep in a rustic trapper cabin or remote expedition field camp.
  * Day 2: Explore coastal icebergs, ice caves, and frozen river canyons on the return route.
* **Approx. Price:** **~9,500 – 12,500 NOK** (~3,600 – 4,700 PLN / ~€825 – €1,080) per person

### 16. 2-Day Pyramiden Ghost Town Snowmobile Expedition (Hotel Tulpan)
* **Operator:** *Poli Arctici*, *Spitzbergen Adventures*, *GoArctica*
* **Duration:** 2 Days / 1 Night (~240 km round-trip)
* **Format:** Ride north across Nordenskiöld Land past the calving front of the Nordenskiöld Glacier into Pyramiden.
* **The Experience:**
  * Guided tour of the abandoned Soviet cultural palace, sports complex, and monument to Lenin.
  * Overnight at the Soviet-style **Hotel Tulpan**, with Russian cuisine, vodka, and an eerie quiet in the deserted settlement under the Midnight Sun.
* **Approx. Price:** **~8,800 – 11,500 NOK** (~3,300 – 4,350 PLN / ~€760 – €990) per person

## 5. Ideal Combinations for a 5–6 Person Mountain Group

### Combo A: "Peak Arctic Wilderness" (Glacier Overnight + East Coast Push)
* **Day 1:** Arrival, gear pickup, Gruve 3 Mine Tour & Svalbard Brewery.
* **Day 2:** 1-Day East Coast Snowmobile Safari (200km push to polar bear pack ice).
* **Day 3–4:** 2-Day "Sleep Inside a Glacier" Ice Cave Overnight on Larsbreen.
* **Day 5:** Duty-free shopping & departure.

### Combo B: "All-Terrain Explorer" (Snowmobiling + Dogsledding + Walrus Boat)
* **Day 1:** Arrival & gear checkout.
* **Day 2:** 1-Day Independent Self-Drive Snowmobile to Fredheim / Sassendalen.
* **Day 3:** Full-Day Dog Sledding across Scott Turnerbreen glacier.
* **Day 4:** Morning Walrus RIB boat safari (Better Moments) + Evening Trollsteinen Ridge Climb (850m) under Midnight Sun.
* **Day 5:** Departure.
